Course purpose

From behavior to computation to circuits

The course integrates psychology, neuroscience, economics, and computational modeling. The lecture slides are the source of truth for the instructional sequence represented here.

🧠

Neural mechanisms

Identify circuits involved in reward, learning, memory, motivation, cognitive control, and choice.

📐

Computational models

Use formal tools such as expected utility, drift diffusion, Rescorla–Wagner, temporal-difference learning, and actor–critic models.

🔬

Evidence

Evaluate behavioral, electrophysiological, lesion, neuroimaging, and causal evidence across species.

🧪

Research design

Translate a course question into a testable hypothesis, web-based behavioral task, analysis, and scientific interpretation.

Publication boundaries

What is—and is not—published

Student privacy and course integrity come first. This site publishes instructional summaries, interactive tools, an anonymous classroom poll, and credited public student projects. It does not publish grades, original submissions, identified student records, participant-level research datasets, exam keys, instructor-only notes, publisher test banks, or copyrighted readings. Student names appear only with their showcased projects.
